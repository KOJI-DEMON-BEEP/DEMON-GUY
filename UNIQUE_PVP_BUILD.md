# GOLDEN AGE FOUNDRY: the tri-path detonator
*Deepwoken PvP, patch 3.2.21a (Oct 2026). Oathless · Kiron · Authority Ensign (Shock Corps) · Ironsing 75 / Thundercall 60 / Frostdraw 50 · Ignition Deepcrusher*

**Pitch:** Your club swings put yellow **Metal Rods** on the enemy. Three homing Fleeting Sparks motes and your Amped swings add blurple **Surge rods**, and your Frostdraw mantras grow pink **crystals**. Then one press of **X** (the Ironsing Pull) sets off all three at once through **Golden Age**. The same Pull forges flying Artisan's Blades, steals their armor and puts their mantra cooldowns under **Sapped**.

The skill is counting. Surge goes off on its own when a 6th rod would land, and since Jul 7 the Overload cooldown is on *you*. So you press X at 4–5 Surge, before a stray hit wastes the Overload.

### Why it's unique
- **Golden Age is the only talent in the data that needs three attunement paths:** Gilded Path: Scrapsinger, Glass Path: Crystallization and Surge Path: Unstable Capacitor. I checked every talent's requirements. The only other multi-path talents are Fulgurite Formation (2 paths, used here) and Volcanic Glass. Its trigger is your Ironsing Pull, which is a talent input and not a mantra. Suffocate only "removes your ability to cast Mantras", so it should not stop the Pull (inference).
- **Compared with the 51 sampled community PvP builds:**
  - No build pairs Ironsing with Frostdraw or Thundercall. Ironsing appears once, alone, on an Iron Requiem Chainwarden.
  - Oathless 3/51, Stormchanter's Raiments 1/51, Wind-Up 1/51.
  - Ignition Deepcrusher 0/51, Kiron 0/51, and the Shock Corps kit never appears.
- **Common-core picks:** it skips Black Diver (25/51), Blood Scourge (34/51), the Charm Caster package, Reinforced Armor, Ghost / Swift Rebound / Evasive Expert, Speed Demon, Spine Cutter, Wyvern's Claw, Shattered Katana, and the Jetstriker and Silentheart oaths. The one common piece is **Ardour** (44/51), used only as a posture toggle for guardbreaks, with no Ardour Scream.
- **Compared with your Stormbreaker Soul:**

| | Stormbreaker Soul | Golden Age Foundry |
|---|---|---|
| Oath | Soulbreaker | **Oathless** |
| Main attunement | Thundercall (Surge + Interrogator) | **Ironsing**, with Frostdraw and Thundercall as feeders |
| Weapon | Stormseye | **Ignition Deepcrusher** |
| Defense | FTD 90 + Reinforced Armor | **Armor theft + armor-state layers:** Polished Armor (armor above 90%), Oath Armor (armor intact), Padded Armor + Steel Scales (armor broken) |
| Win condition | Interrogator Electrified + Charm crits + Ardour Scream | **Pull detonation** + Scrapsinger blade loop + Sapped |
| Murmur | All three (oath) + Ardour Scream | Ardour toggle only |
| Race / Origin / Outfit | Vesperian / Lone Warrior / Celtorian Tideknight | **Kiron / Authority Ensign (Shock Corps) / Stormchanter's Raiments** |

  - **Honest overlap (all support pieces):**
    - The Surge package: Surge Path, Closed Circuit, Fried Circuits, Catalytic Strike.
    - Electrify + Amplified Rage, and Static Link (here it only switches on Frozen Web).
    - Gear and gems: Warmaster's Medallion, Bloodless Gem, Insignia Gem, Vitality.
    - Generic defensive cards: Perseverance, Disbelief, Old Habits Die Hard, Neuroplasticity, Armor Conserver, Padded Armor, Steel Scales, Safety Dance, Risky Moves, Braced Collapse, Bulldozer, Ether Absorption.
  - **No overlap** in the identity pieces: Golden Age, the Scrapsinger tree, Glass Path, Fulgurite Formation, Frozen Web, the Shock Corps talents, Oathless, the Deepcrusher, the Chained Ring + Saint Jay's Ring uppercut kit.

### How points arrive
- The Power page in the pack says: "Each level of Power grants 15 investment points". You put them in one at a time as you earn EXP, and you Power up once all 15 are in.
- The README's 330 at Power 20 includes 5 racial points. 330 − 5 − 15×20 = 25, so the pack implies 25 starting points.
- So you hold about **15 + 15×N points (incl. race) when you reach Power N**, and 30 + 15×N when you finish it. Examples: 135 on reaching Power 8, 150 on reaching Power 9, 315 on reaching Power 20, 330 at the end.
- Whisper Draws are offered as you Power up, so the checkpoints below use the "on reaching" number.

- **When:** you reach Power 9 holding 150. Put in 7 more to reach 157, then use the shrine (Power 8+, 10 Knowledge, no Oath yet).
- Every core stat loses 25 or less (the largest drop is WLL 35 → 17), so everything lands on 17 and you get 4 points back.
- **Racial points:** the pack doesn't say whether the shrine averages Kiron's +3/+2. If it doesn't, the invested-only run is `{"WLL":35,"FTD":35,"STR":30,"AGL":25,"INT":23,…}` → **all 16, 8 refunded**. Kiron's points then put AGL at 19 and INT at 18, both still ≤ final. Legal either way.

### Route B: no flasks until after the shrine
**Don't** use the shrine with only 7 stats after building the full Route A spread. shrine.py gives all **22** (1 refunded), and the final spread no longer fits: 4×22 + 35 + 40 + 75 + 60 + 50 = 348 > 330.

Instead, use the shrine **the moment you reach Power 8** (135 points), with INT left low:

- **What Route B keeps:** everything in the identity: Golden Age, Fulgurite, Fried Circuits, Human Battery, Iceberg, Polished Armor, the Deepcrusher and Stormchanter's. All pre-shrine talents are still reachable.
- **What Route B loses:** Neuroplasticity (and with it the Enforcer Pull slot), Windup Battery and Eureka (INT 30). Max ether drops to 178.
- **Timing:** about half a Power to one Power behind Route A. Golden Age comes a few points into Power 14, the Deepcrusher a few points into Power 18, and Stormchanter's and Fulgurite during Power 19. If the Thundercall flask isn't in by Power 10, swap that draw for an Ironsing mantra.

- **HP:**

## 6. Gameplay

### Four hard rules (read these first)
1. **Count Surge, and never let the 6th land on its own.** Surge Overloads when 5 rods are on and a 6th would apply. The Overload cooldown is on you (Jul 7), so a stray M1 or mote that Overloads them means your next Pull can't. **Press X at 4–5 Surge.** Surge sources: each Fleeting Sparks mote (≤3), each Amped weapon hit (M1, uppercut, crit), and possibly each Iron Tether hit.
2. **Crystals are allowed to pop on their own.** No crystal cooldown is documented, and a natural pop still fires Fulgurite's lightning strike. You only lose them from the next Pull.
3. **The Pull needs at least 1 metal rod.** Crits (Songs Unforged), flourishes (Gilded Path) and Corrupt Wind-Up eat *every* rod. Don't crit or flourish right before X unless you rebuild a rod first (club M1, Needle Barrage, Metal Ball, a Masterwork blade).
4. **Electrify after the first X.** Amped M1s load Surge too, so if you're Amped during LOAD you'll Overload them by accident. During RELOAD your Overload is cooling down anyway, so that's when Amped double-loading is free.

### The engine loop: LOAD → CASH → RELOAD → CRIT → rebuild → CASH … → SIPHON
1. **LOAD (not Amped):**
   - Fleeting Sparks (Magnet): ≤3 Surge.
   - Club M1s: yellow rods. 3+ rods means Heavy Shoulders shortens their rolls.
   - Uppercut: Saint Jay crystal, Static Link, Chained Ring lock, Dazed Band chance, and probably a rod (if uppercuts count as club basic attacks).
   - Crystal Knee: crystals.
2. **CASH (X):** the Golden Age Pull does all of this at once:
   - Crystals explode, and a Fulgurite lightning strike follows.
   - Surge Overloads, with +6.5 from Closed Circuit.
   - **Sapped for 5s** (Fried Circuits).
   - Metal Thief steals armor, and Artisan's Blades are forged (1 per 2 rods).
   - **Fulgurite's 8s window opens:** your crystals apply twice.
3. **RELOAD (inside the 8s window):**
   - **Electrify now.** Amped M1s plant 1 metal + 1 Surge each. Stop adding Surge at 5 until your Overload is back.
   - Ice Lasers (Spring + Cloudstone) rebuilds crystals at double speed. If they pop on their own, fine (rule 2).
   - Your next Ironsing attack fires the blades, and Masterwork makes them plant rods and deal +50%.
4. **CRIT (only with rods on them):** one crit does five things:
   - **Songs Unforged:** Scrapsinger eats the rods, siphons armor, starts Refine and Reuse's PEN cut, forges blades and adds a Reshape and Remold stack.
   - **Frozen Web:** a crystal.
   - **Blind Spot:** Blinded 5s.
   - **Bear Trap:** no jumping for 4s.
   - **Warmaster's Medallion** brings the next crit back sooner.
5. **Rebuild ≥1 rod** (Metal Ball / Needle Barrage fires the blades, and Masterwork plants rods), then **X again** once your Overload cooldown is back (§7, test 3).
6. **SIPHON (flourish last):**
   - Gilded Path eats the remaining rods and siphons their armor.
   - Dazed Band has a chance to stun.
   - Into a wall: Bulldozer may guardbreak, which triggers Chain 5.

**Timing constraints that shape this order:**
- Uppercut has a **3s cooldown** (Apr 5) and swings at 0.95× (Sep 9).
- Static Link lasts **15s and can't be refreshed**.
- The Pull, crits and flourishes all consume rods. Artisan's Blade counts "rods you consume" and procs on a Pull.
- So: **uppercut first** (Static Link + Saint Jay crystal + Chained Ring + Dragon's Song) → load → **X** → Electrify + reload → crit → rebuild → **X** → **flourish last**.

**Honest read:**
- Each burst is small. The build wins on **volume** (three charge types refilling at the same time), **denial** (Sapped, Shackled, no-jump, no-air-dash, Blinded) and **attrition** (their armor becomes yours).
- At 10 Reshape and Remold stacks (+5%) and once their armor is gone (Exposed Durability, +10%), everything hits harder.

**Charged Ring vs Dazed Band (4th ring):**
- Charged Ring lowers the charges needed for crystal explosions and Overloads, but "also reduces the damage they deal" (amount undocumented).
- With rule 1, a lower Surge threshold means more accidental Overloads before X, so it works against this loop.
- So **Dazed Band** is the pick. If test 2 (§7) shows Golden Age only fires on full stacks, X becomes your "6th hit": press it at exactly 5 Surge. Only then is Charged Ring worth testing.

### Defensive plan
- **One layer for each armor state:**
  - Armor above 90%: **Polished Armor** (8.75%).
  - Armor intact: **Oathless Oath Armor** (20%), only against **pure Oath damage**. It's *armor*, so PEN reduces it, and attacks that mix damage types skip it. Pure-Oath kits per the wiki: Blindseer, Contractor, Chainwarden, Dawnwalker, Fadetrimmer, Jetstriker, Starkindred (except Death From Above), and Soulbreaker's Ardour Slicer. **Silentheart lost its Oath damage on Sep 28.**
  - Armor broken: **Padded Armor + Steel Scales** (10%).
  - Keeping armor up: Metal Thief (≤2% of their current armor per rod; Aug 13 nerf), Gilded siphon, Reshape and Remold (rod drain 2.5%), Armorer's Ring (−25% loss), Armor Conserver (−15%). The pack doesn't give the armor-loss-per-hit formula, so I can't prove you stay above 90% under constant pressure. Expect it at fight start and right after siphon bursts.
- **Near-constant:** **Aegis Gem** 10% for 10s, refreshed every time Fleeting Sparks lands (10s cooldown).
- **On a dodge:** Safety Dance widens the window, **Risky Moves** auto-dodges the next attack, and **An Ironsinger's Instinct** gives 60% reduction for 2s.
- **When you get guardbroken:** **Braced Collapse** (−20% on the next hit) and **Instant Nucleation** (amount undocumented). Ardour's −15% posture taken makes it happen less.
- **On Scrapsinger procs:** Refine and Reuse cuts incoming PEN by about 23% with 5 rods, for 14s.
- **After your guardbreak (Amp Overdrive), 7s:** their basic attacks become Thundercall (Stormchanter's 25% thunder armor) and deal 10% less. Mantras and oath moves are not converted.
- **Recovery:**
  - Iceberg: heal pulses and an autoparry frame.
  - Electrify heal (Casque, ≈12).
  - Bloodless Gem on Needle Barrage (≤15 HP per proc).
  - The Eleventh Hour, Perseverance.
- **Knowing the trade-offs:** **Amped raises the damage you take by 10%.** Only pop Electrify for RELOAD, not while you're trading.

### Open-world plan
- **Echolocator** pings anyone unsheathing nearby, even through Tacet and Lowstride, so third parties can't sneak up.
- **Corrupt Wind-Up** counts as a flourish: Gilded siphon + Static Link + Dazed Band. It eats rods, so use it **after** X. It executes at 1 charge, so you can finish knocks fast.
- **Warmaster's Medallion** cuts crit cooldown 20% outside Chime, but also makes your resonance cooldown 20% longer. That slows Wind-Up; it's the price of the crit loop.
- **Abyssal Tendrils** pierces several targets.
- **Open-world extras:** Brassneck (20% damage resistance for 15s after a knock), Comeback Kid, Polite Awakening, Impervious Slumber.
- **Oathless's 20% Monster Armor** helps in PvE.
- **Option:** take **Chronostasis** if offered. Your hits put their Resonance on a 13s cooldown, which counters Blood Scourge (34/51 sampled builds).

   - Crossguard adds +10% chip (sword vs sword).

2. **Jetstriker (Galebreathe):**
   - Jet Kick is pure Oath damage, so Oath Armor works while your armor holds.
   - Suffocate blocks your mantras but not your **Pull**, so keep charges stacked before they choke you.
   - Sapped lengthens their mantra cooldowns.

3. **Charm / Soulbreaker / Interrogator (Stormbreaker Soul-style):**
   - Disbelief halves Charm. Ardour Slicer is pure Oath damage (Oath Armor).
   - If their Interrogator Electrifies you, your M1s lose damage. Lean on talent damage instead: detonations, blades and Closed Circuit.
   - Their Thundercall runs into Stormchanter's 25% thunder armor.
4. **Chainwarden / Contractor / Starkindred (pure Oath damage):**
   - Oath Armor applies while your armor is intact.
   - Fried Circuits Sapped stretches their mantra cooldowns; the pack doesn't say whether that includes oath mantras.
   - Enforcer Pull and Iron Tether stop Chainwarden from kiting.
5. **Dawnwalker:**
   - **Absolute Radiance:** after they deal Dawnwalker mantra damage, they're immune to Chill, Shock, Blindness and Metal Rods for 7s (12s cooldown). It **doesn't clear stacks already on them.**
   - So stack rods, Surge and crystals *before* their first Dawnwalker mantra lands, and Pull or detonate right away.
   - During the immunity, use raw-damage mantras (Abyssal Tendrils, Grand Warden's Axe) and posture for a guardbreak. Reload in the ~5s gap before Radiance can proc again.
   - Sapped stretches their mantra cooldowns, which delays the next Radiance. Their oath mantras are pure Oath damage (Oath Armor).
6. **FTD 90 / Reinforced Armor tanks:**
   - Your PEN gets cut, so shred their armor instead: Piercing Metal (+5% armor damage per rod), Reshape and Remold, Metal Thief.
   - Then Exposed Durability (+10%) finishes them.
7. **Mirror Ironsing / Iron Requiem:**
   - Ironsinger Heavy Plate adds 5% Ironsing armor, and most Ironsing mantras deal only Metal damage now (Aug 13).
   - Thornmail punishes their flourishes, and your Pull steals their armor before theirs steals yours.
   - Shocking Reverb would also negate Ironsing damage (bonus).
8. **Flamecharm casters:**
   - Iceberg heals out burn windows. The Aegis Gem and the armor-state layers carry you.
   - Their fire isn't converted by Electrified (mantras keep their type), so don't expect the thunder armor to help.

### Weaknesses
- About 300–330 HP, no Fortitude layer, and Amped's +10% incoming damage.
- Every mitigation layer is conditional on armor state, a dodge, a gem proc or a guardbreak. The Aegis Gem is the closest thing to always-on, and it costs a Doom of Caeranthil's Scale.
- Mantras sit at L3–L4 (Crystal Knee 41, Ice Lasers 14, Iron Tether 15.58).
- Each detonation is small (≈76 raw for a full Pull). You need time in the loop.
- You have to **count**: Surge ≤5 before X, ≥1 rod before X, no crit before X. The Overload cooldown is on you (Jul 7), and its length isn't documented.
- No air dash (Chained Ring).
- Hard-to-get pieces: Mythical Deepcrusher (no drop info), Shock Corps Division, Charged Parasol Part, Enforcer Eye, the Gaunt Man's experiments plus a Doom of Caeranthil's Scale, two Attunement Flasks.
- Dawnwalker's Radiance can blank your charge loading for 7s at a time.
- Several links are inferred, not documented (see §7).

## 8. Variants and alternatives
- **No Ignition Deepcrusher:**
  - **Weapon:** **Alloyed Cavalry Saber** (Sword, 22.5 base, MED 5.5, 10% PEN, posture 6, enchantable). Its requirement is "MED 75 **or** Authority Ensign origin", so your origin unlocks it. Use the **Chilling** enchant: "If the wielder has Crystallization, apply an Ice Crystal instead" of Bottom Freeze, every 4 medium attacks, 5s per-victim cooldown.
  - **Damage:** M1 ≈26.2 at MED 40 (Deepcrusher 23.7; plain Cavalry Saber 24.2).
  - **What changes:** M1s no longer plant metal rods, but Amped still plants Surge. Rods come from Needle Barrage, Metal Ball, Masterwork blades and Thornmail, so rule 3 matters more.
  - Keep Ironsing 75 for Polished Armor and Instinct.
- **No Shock Corps Division:**
  - Pick Castaway or Lone Warrior instead.
  - You lose Amp Overdrive, Shocking Reverb, Martial Brutality, Steel Tread, Crossguard and Grand Warden's Axe.
  - Put **Thunder Kick** (LTN 20, 1★; L4 34 damage / 25 posture; GB 5) in the Combat slot and keep the Might Gem.
  - Refill the talents with Chronostasis, Cryonis, Chilling Flourish or Champion's Regalia (STR 25, pre-shrine).
  - Guardbreaks still pop crystals on their own, and Catalytic Strike still Overloads.
- **Polished Armor rarely above 90%, or no Stormchanter's:** **Shock Corps Light** (Authority / Shock Corps, Power 15; 3500 durability; 30 physical / 30 elemental / 10 thunder; Battle-Hardened: −20% damage from mantra guardbreaks). The extra durability helps Polished Armor and Oath Armor stay up. Without Circuit Breaker, Electrify costs 5% of your current HP; Deepscorn's Already Dead reduces that.
- **No monster parts:**
  - Instead of Abyssal Tendrils: **Iron Slam** (MTL 50, 3★, L4 46.15 / 16, GB 5).
  - Instead of Enforcer Pull: **Chain Pull** (MTL 20).
  - Each costs a draw, so you get one less Roll 2.
- **Want Shocking Reverb and Windup Battery more often:** swap Enforcer Pull for **Lightning Impact** (LTN 20, 1★; L4 27.5 / 10; GB 5). It's a second pure Thundercall mantra, which Stormbreaker Soul doesn't run. It costs one Roll 2 and the Tether Cage pull.
- **Golden Age needs full stacks:** press X at exactly 5 Surge. Charged Ring then becomes worth testing in place of Dazed Band.
- **Frostbite / Saint Jay turn out dead under Glass Path:** take **Cryonis** + **Chilling Flourish** (FRD 30: flourishes leave ice patches) instead.
- **No Gaunt Man's experiments or no Scale:** Fleeting Sparks takes a Blue Gem. You lose the near-constant 10%, so lean harder on dodges.
- **Neuroplasticity doesn't add a slot:** the talent text in the pack only lists a 10% modifier discount; the slot comes from the README rule. If you don't get it, drop Enforcer Pull. (Route B drops it anyway.)
- **Off-meta murmur:** **Rhythm** instead of Ardour (tracking and health reads; from Kadrivus Entomolius Auditan's quest in the Second Layer). You lose Ardour's posture help on guardbreaks.
- **Chime-only extra HP:** **Armored Plating** (Authority Ensign): 50 temp HP each time combat starts, but permanently slower (amount undocumented).
- **More M1 damage:** Proficiency 6 instead of Songchant 6. M1 goes to ≈25.9 (≈31 Amped) and weapon PEN +15%. You give up the 15% mantra PEN and the monster-mantra bonus.
